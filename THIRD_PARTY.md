## Policy for future vendoring (stage 09 especially)

- Before vendoring any retro CPU core (65C816, ARM7TDMI-compatible,
  SM83), record its license here first. GPL cores make derived RTL
  GPL — acceptable, but it must be a decision, not an accident.
  "ARM7TDMI-compatible" cores also carry trademark/cleanroom caveats;
  prefer cores with a clear provenance statement.
- **No commercial ROMs, BIOS images, or proprietary console assets**
  are included in this repository, ever. Test software is written
  in-repo or generated by in-repo tools.
- Game/tile/audio assets committed here must be original or under a
  license permitting redistribution, recorded in this file.
